    What is Rotaract?

    Young people who act, connect and lead

    Rotaract is an international network of young people who want to make a positive impact on their community, develop leadership skills and be part of an active, committed community. At Rotaract Club Igualada, young people aged 18 to 30, we bring this spirit to our city and the Anoia region.

    Service

    Projects designed to respond to real needs with direct impact.

    Leadership

    Personal growth, shared responsibility and youth initiative.

    Community

    Bonds, friendship and networking to grow together and gain strength.

    Come and discover who we are, what we do and how you can take part. No previous experience needed: just the will to contribute, learn and meet people who share your interests.

    Your Rotary area of focus

    🕊️

    Promoting peace

    You bring people together: you care about living side by side and solving conflicts by talking.

    Peace

    Your strengths

    • Empathy
    • Dialogue
    • Open-mindedness

    How you can contribute through the club

    • Organising intercultural meetings and community activities
    • Suggesting anti-bullying workshops in secondary schools
    • Meeting young people from other countries at district and international events

    What Rotary does in this area

    Fostering understanding between people of different cultures, supporting refugees and training adults and young people to prevent and mediate conflict.

    Did you know… Every year, Rotary offers up to 170 fully funded fellowships to study peace and conflict resolution at its 8 Peace Centers, at universities around the world.

    Source: rotary.org

    💉

    Fighting disease

    You care about everyone's health: preventing before curing, and knowing how to act when it matters.

    Disease

    Your strengths

    • Caring for others
    • Responsibility
    • Calm under pressure

    How you can contribute through the club

    • Organising blood donation drives
    • Promoting first-aid and CPR workshops
    • Setting up charity runs and challenges for research

    What Rotary does in this area

    Educating and equipping communities to stop the spread of serious diseases, and working so that everyone has access to basic health care.

    Did you know… Rotary launched its PolioPlus programme in 1985 and, since 1988, polio cases worldwide have fallen by more than 99.9%.

    Source: endpolio.org (Rotary)

    🚰

    Providing clean water, sanitation and hygiene

    You know water is life: you care that everyone has clean, safe water.

    Water

    Your strengths

    • Practical sense
    • Consistency
    • Global outlook

    How you can contribute through the club

    • Suggesting clean-ups of rivers and springs
    • Running water-saving campaigns
    • Collaborating on Rotary water projects in other countries

    What Rotary does in this area

    Working with communities to provide clean water, improve sanitation and promote hygiene.

    Did you know… In schools, Rotary installs clean water and toilets and trains teachers to teach hygiene habits.

    Source: rotary.org

    👶

    Saving mothers and children

    You care about the little ones: that every baby and every mother has what they need.

    Mothers & children

    Your strengths

    • Tenderness
    • Protective instinct
    • Patience

    How you can contribute through the club

    • Collecting clothes, nappies and supplies for families with babies
    • Working with organisations that support young mothers
    • Organising activities with children and families

    What Rotary does in this area

    Connecting mothers and young children with the nutrition, health care and sanitation they need.

    Did you know… In Nigeria, a Rotary programme trains health workers and upgrades clinics: in two years, the facilities taking part reported 20% fewer maternal deaths and 28% fewer newborn deaths.

    Source: rotary.org

    📚

    Supporting education

    You believe in the power of learning: you care that everyone gets the chance to learn.

    Education

    Your strengths

    • Curiosity
    • Patience
    • Love of teaching

    How you can contribute through the club

    • Tutoring or running workshops in schools and youth clubs
    • Organising book and school supply drives
    • Sharing what you know in workshops for young people

    What Rotary does in this area

    Helping communities provide basic education, reduce the gender gap in schooling and help more adults learn to read and write.

    Did you know… In Afghanistan, Rotary members opened a school for girls to break the cycle of poverty.

    Source: rotary.org

    💼

    Growing local economies

    You're all about ideas and opportunities: you care that everyone can get ahead through their work.

    Local economies

    Your strengths

    • Initiative
    • Creativity
    • Entrepreneurial spirit

    How you can contribute through the club

    • Organising job talks and workshops for young people
    • Supporting local shops with campaigns
    • Helping entrepreneurs get their projects off the ground

    What Rotary does in this area

    Training, business advice, financial education and access to small loans so that entrepreneurs can thrive.

    Did you know… In Esmeraldas (Ecuador), Rotary members helped arrange more than 250 microloans and trained over 270 people in trades and business skills.

    Source: rotary.org

    🌍

    Protecting the environment

    You care about the planet: you want to leave the world better than you found it.

    Environment

    Your strengths

    • Environmental awareness
    • Consistency
    • Drive to take action

    How you can contribute through the club

    • Suggesting and leading clean-ups and tree planting
    • Making the club's activities produce less waste
    • Building partnerships with environmental groups

    What Rotary does in this area

    Conserving and protecting natural resources: restoring land, rivers and seas, reducing waste and tackling the causes of climate change.

    Did you know… It is Rotary's seventh area of focus: its governing bodies approved it unanimously in 2020.

    Source: rotary.org
