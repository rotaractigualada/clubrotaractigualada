What is Rotaract?

Young people who act, connect and lead

Rotaract is an international network of young people who want to make a positive impact on their community, develop leadership skills and be part of an active, committed community. At Rotaract Club Igualada, young people aged 18 to 30, we bring this spirit to our city and the Anoia region.

Service

Projects designed to respond to real needs with direct impact.

Leadership

Personal growth, shared responsibility and youth initiative.

Community

Bonds, friendship and networking to grow together and gain strength.

Come and discover who we are, what we do and how you can take part. No previous experience needed: just the will to contribute, learn and meet people who share your interests.
