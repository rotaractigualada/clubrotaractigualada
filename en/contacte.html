Join the club

How to join the club

Want to be part of Rotaract Igualada? The process is simple and transparent.

  1. 1

    Fill in the form

    Tell us who you are, what your values are, and why you want to be part of the club.

  2. 2

    Application review

    The club team will review your application to better understand your profile and motivations.

  3. 3

    Club meetings

    You will attend two club meetings to meet the members, see how we work, and decide if you're a good fit.

  4. 4

    Final vote

    After the meetings, members will conduct an anonymous vote to decide on your admission.

  5. 5

    Response within 48 hours

    You will be notified by email within 48 hours with the result of the vote. Whether it's a yes or a not yet, we'll be by your side to guide your next steps.

Ready to get started?

We will get back to you as soon as possible.

Rotaract Igualada

We are a community of committed young people driving social and leadership projects in the Anoia region. As part of the global Rotary network, we turn our energy into community service and real action. We bring together talent, friendship and values to create a positive impact and grow together.
